Automated Inventory Reconciliation System – SAP EWM & WES

Tech Stack: Microsoft Office Scripts, Excel, SAP EWM, WES (Systore), Pivot Tables, Data Automation

Developed a one-click automated inventory reconciliation system using Microsoft Office Scripts to compare Handling Units (HUs) between SAP-EWM and Systore WES based on unique HU numbers. The automation identifies and separates unmatched inventory from both systems, copies the exceptions into dedicated sheets, and generates automated Pivot Tables to provide crane-wise inventory visibility across Crane 1, Crane 2, and Crane 3. Improved exception identification and reconciliation visibility, enabling faster investigation and correction of inventory discrepancies. Reduced daily reconciliation time from approximately 1–1.3 hours to just 2–5 minutes.
